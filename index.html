The Brand

Clothing with a Passport

Flytz Co. began with a simple idea: the places we travel change the way we dress, and the way we see. Each capsule is designed around one destination — its monuments, materials, street light and skyline — then cut in limited runs.

From the iron lattice of Paris to the neon grid of Tokyo, every stitch carries coordinates. Collect the world, one garment at a time.
